- Only target relevant, high-quality websites
- Avoid link farms and spam directories
- Monitor backlink quality regularly

### Anchor Text Diversity:
- Use natural, varied anchor text
- Avoid over-optimization
- Focus on brand and generic terms

### Compliance:
- Follow Google Webmaster Guidelines
- Avoid paid link schemes
- Maintain natural link profile
