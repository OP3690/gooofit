## Risk Mitigation

### Quality Control
- Only pursue backlinks from reputable sources
- Avoid link farms and spammy directories
- Focus on editorial/natural links
- Regular backlink audits

### Compliance
- Follow Google's Webmaster Guidelines
- Avoid paid link schemes
- Maintain natural link velocity
- Monitor for toxic backlinks
